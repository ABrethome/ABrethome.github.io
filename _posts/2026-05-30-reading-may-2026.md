**Title: Six Choices Every AI Engineer Has to Make (and Nobody Teaches)**

[Link](https://towardsdatascience.com/six-choices-every-ai-engineer-has-to-make-and-nobody-teaches/)

Summary: The article explains six key production trade‑offs AI engineers face but aren't taught: build vs buy (API vs self‑hosting), model complexity vs maintainability, data quantity vs quality, batch vs real‑time inference, prompt engineering vs fine‑tuning, and automation vs human oversight. It emphasizes that AI engineering is about balancing cost, scalability, and reliability, not just model accuracy.

**Title: Context Engineering for AI Agents: Complete Course**

[Link](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/context-engineering-for-ai-agents-complete-course-fd509e276a9e)

Summary: The article presents a “complete course” on context engineering, the practice of managing everything an AI agent sees (prompts, history, tool outputs, memory) across multi-step tasks. It explains why agents fail due to poor context, not weak models, and introduces core strategies to structure, filter, and maintain context, prevent degradation over time, and design reliable, production-grade agent systems.

**Title: We built SmithDB, the data layer for agent observability**

[Link](https://www.langchain.com/blog/introducing-smithdb)

Summary: The article introduces SmithDB, a distributed database built by LangChain for AI agent observability. It’s designed to handle complex agent traces (multi-step, multimodal, long-running workflows) that traditional databases struggle with. SmithDB enables fast querying, scaling, and analysis of agent behavior, helping teams debug, monitor, and improve AI systems in production more efficiently.

**Title: How to Build an Efficient Knowledge Base for AI Models**

[Link](https://towardsdatascience.com/how-to-build-an-efficient-knowledge-base-for-ai-models/)

Summary: The article explains how to build an effective knowledge base for AI models using a structured, iterative approach. It emphasizes prioritizing high‑quality, relevant data over volume, cleaning and chunking content into meaningful units, adding metadata, and enabling efficient retrieval (e.g., via embeddings). A well-designed knowledge base improves accuracy, speed, and reduces hallucinations in AI systems.

**Title: AI co-mathematician: Accelerating mathematicians with agentic AI**

[Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06651)

Summary: The paper introduces an "AI co‑mathematician", an agent-based workspace that collaborates with researchers on open-ended math problems. It supports the full research workflow (ideation, literature search, exploration, theorem proving) using a stateful, iterative system that tracks uncertainty and failed hypotheses. It improves discovery and achieves state-of-the-art results on hard benchmarks (e.g., FrontierMath). This architecture could be re-used on other topics than Math.

---

**Title: Agentic AI: How to Save on Tokens**

[Link](https://towardsdatascience.com/agentic-ai-how-to-save-on-tokens/)

Summary: The article explains how agentic AI systems become expensive due to growing context and repeated token processing, and outlines strategies to reduce cost. Key techniques include prompt and semantic caching (reuse tokens), lazy-loading tools/MCPs, routing tasks to smaller models, delegating to subagents, and cleaning/compacting context to avoid unnecessary token usage.
